About

I help brands find the one thing worth saying, and then say it everywhere.

I'm Dania, a brand and content strategist based in Mérida. For eight years I've worked with food, hospitality and wellness brands that had a good product and a muddled story.

I work across strategy, social and campaigns, usually with small teams that need a clear voice more than a bigger budget.
