Dania wants to work on her projects: $ARGUMENTS

1. Show the current projects as a numbered list of titles. If it is not clear which action
   she wants, ask: add, edit, remove or move?
2. Gather only what is missing, one question at a time.
   - Add: kind of work, title, where and when (place, dates, or both), one sentence about
     it, and its position in the list. Then offer, as yes or no: a few "What I did" points,
     and a result with a number (a short number and what it measures). Both are optional.
     A new project gets a cover; a photo for it is JC's (offer the message).
   - Edit: which project, and the new wording.
   - Remove: which project. Quote its title and text in the restatement.
   - Move: which project, and its new position. Show the list in its new order.
   Never fill a gap with invented facts. If she has no number, the project goes on without
   one.
3. Follow "The steps for every change" in AGENTS.md, from step 1, including the wait.
   After adding, removing or moving, set the covers again in order: none, `b`, `c`, `d`,
   and repeat, counting only projects without a photo. Say in the restatement that covers
   may change. A photo always stays with its own project.
